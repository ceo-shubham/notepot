About NotePot Handwriting Formatter

NotePot is a specialized document formatter designed to render typed text into authentic ruled Hindi and English handwritten notebook pages. It solves the common alignment problem where Devanagari characters drift across ruled notebook lines by employing exact baseline lock geometry.

Devanagari Ruled Alignment

Line spacing is locked on ruled pages to preserve natural Shirorekha hanging alignment, while letter and word spacing remain customizable.

38+ Handwriting Fonts

Includes verified Devanagari scripts (Kalam, Tillana, Dekko, Amita) and Latin scripts (Caveat, Shadows Into Light, Patrick Hand).

Direct PDF Export

Exports high-resolution multi-page A4 PDFs with preserved ink styling, margins, and paper headers for academic submission.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I export my finished assignment?

Click the "Download PDF" button in the top navigation. NotePot compiles all pages into a single high-definition A4 PDF file ready for printing or digital submission.

Can I write both Hindi and English on the same page?

Yes. Fonts such as Kalam, Tillana, and Dekko support both Devanagari and Latin character sets, allowing mixed Hindi, English, and numerical content.

How is my document data saved?

Assignments are saved locally in browser storage and synchronized to encrypted Backblaze B2 cloud storage when you click "Save" or sign in with your Google account.
